What is meeMail?

meeMail is the official messaging platform of the mee ecosystem. It gives every meeian a unique @mee.com address that works across all mee services — no external email accounts, no spam, just seamless, encrypted communication between verified members.

Why meeMail?

End-to-End Encrypted

Every message is secured. Only you and the recipient can read it.

One Identity

Your @mee.com address is your passport to all mee products.

Zero External Dependencies

No Gmail, Yahoo, or any third-party. meeMail runs entirely on its own network.

Smart Inbox

Clean, focused interface for your conversations. No ads, no distractions.
